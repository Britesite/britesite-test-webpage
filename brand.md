## Brand Positioning

**Positioning:** The neighbourhood café where everything on the counter was baked that morning, and where you can just turn up: a window seat, coffee with free refills and a piece of today's cake, at prices you can see before you come. *(synthesised from the facts in this file)*

**Advantages, from the facts only:** baked on site every morning; prices published; open six days with long weekend mornings; something gluten-free and something vegan every day; room for small parties on weekdays.

**Messaging rules:** answer the visitor's three questions before anything else (open now? what does it cost? will there be room?); say "bagt i morges", not "artisan" or "håndlavet med kærlighed"; never invent reviews or awards; the weekend cinnamon swirls are the one thing allowed to be a little proud.

Inputs, in the owner's words:
- What they do: "Vi er en lille kvartercafé i Odense med morgenmad, frokost og kage, vi bager hver morgen." *(intake, 2026-11-14)*

## Tone of Voice — Principles

- Address: **Du** *(intake, 2026-11-14)*
- The owner's own sentence, the best sample of how they talk: "Vi er en lille kvartercafé i Odense med morgenmad, frokost og kage, vi bager hver morgen." *(intake, 2026-11-14)*
- **Plain and warm:** short sentences, everyday words, the way the owner talks at the counter ("Vi bager hver morgen").
- **Concrete over cosy:** show the hygge with facts (the window seat, free refills, the smell at 8) rather than saying "hyggelig".
- **Local without postcards:** name the street and the neighbourhood, not "the heart of Odense".
- **Useful first:** hours, prices and seats are said plainly and early; nothing makes the reader hunt.

**Per language:**
- **Danish:** du-form, lower-key humour, no anglicisms ("take-away" is fine, it is what Danes say); prices as "45 kr.", times as "8–17".
